How To Play Staruu7 Games

Basic Game Settings

Two to five players usually play Teen Patti, with each player holding three cards. With the exception of the little king, it employs two set decks of cards, for a total of 106 cards.

Fundamental Game Rules

Three hands are handed to each participant, and the larger hand wins after the cards are compared based on the type of hand.

Types of Cards

  1. Trail, Trio, or Set (three individuals of the same rank)
  2. Straight Flush, Pure Sequence, or Pure Run (three cards in the same suit in a row)
  3. Run/Straight/Sequence (three cards in a row, not all in the same suit)
  4. Color/Flush (three identical cards, not in order)
  5. Pair/Two of a Kind (two identically ranked cards)
  6. High Card: three cards that don't fit into any of the combinations listed above

Ante Betting

Each player puts a certain amount into the pot, which is the starting wager. After shuffling the deck, the dealer deals each player three cards, face down.
